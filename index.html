Number Patterns

Mathematics 600 Fundamentals - Unit 1
60 randomized questions per attempt. The numbers, question order, and answer choices change each time.

Practice includes vocabulary, term position/value, describing patterns, arithmetic sequences, addition/subtraction/multiplication/division patterns, next terms, and missing terms.

Feedback stays hidden until Submit Quiz is clicked. After submission, students see their score, the correct answer, and an explanation for every question.
